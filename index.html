More About Me

I'm a Data Scientist and Analytics Professional who transforms complex data into actionable insights, predictive solutions, and smarter business decisions. My professional experience spans pricing analytics, business intelligence, machine learning, and data engineering across industries for 3 years. Bridging advanced data science and practical strategy, I turn complex datasets into clear, automated solutions that streamline operations, drive critical decisions, and illuminate hidden patterns in real-world social systems.

Journey to Data Science,

My journey into data science began in 2021, when my background in mathematics and programming led me from C++, Java, MATLAB, and web development toward a bigger question: what can data tell us? Freelancing taught me to listen to clients, understand their needs, and turn technical ideas into practical solutions. Curious about machine learning and AI, I taught myself Python, SQL, R, and statistical modeling through courses, certifications, and hands-on projects.

As my interests grew, I pursued an MS in Applied Statistics with a specialization in Business Analytics at BGSU, strengthening my understanding of statistical inference, experimental design, predictive modeling, and machine learning. Alongside my studies, I applied these concepts in industry, from building business intelligence solutions at Data Crafters to improving institutional analytics at BGSU and exploring customer segmentation and pricing strategies at Hexion. Working with real business problems taught me that building accurate models is only part of the process; understanding business needs and communicating findings to stakeholders matters just as much. My work in healthcare AI and mortgage lending disparities further expanded my perspective, inspiring me to use data science not only for business decisions but also to understand complex societal challenges.
